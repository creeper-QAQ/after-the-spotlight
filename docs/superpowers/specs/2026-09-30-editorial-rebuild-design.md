# 《被看见之后》V0.8：把热闹接到日常

## Intent and authorization
User requests a bold, autonomous upgrade of the existing data-news entry, including independently finding public evidence, strengthening narrative and fixing the issues in the 78/100 review. The target is a credible, memorable competition entry; no award can be promised. The user explicitly authorizes execution without repeated design gates and relies on archives for rollback.

## Scope
Preserve the original fish-lantern overture, paper/vermilion/forest palette, source dialogs and four-stage editorial spine. Shift the promise from measuring an unavailable conversion rate to investigating what public records can prove about attention, participation and the people who continue making/teaching. Public source research adds dated metrics and named learning/teaching paths. Every unknown remains unknown.

## Editorial approach
Recommended: a compact evidence-led main reading with three findings, one interactive comparison of evidence units, and real named people. Alternative all-data dashboard loses the authored story; alternative effects-led expansion preserves the reviewed weakness. Choose the authored story plus optional research annexes.

1. Opening: identify Anhui fish lanterns and Huizhou ink, and say attention is observable while continuation must be traced through people.
2. Three findings: attention has evidence; participation has evidence and commercial/teaching conditions; named makers have continuing paths, but the retention rate of a visitor cohort remains unknown. Each finding links to supporting records.
3. Attention: 17.1 billion is a keyword-video aggregate, never unique viewers or a single village. Decorative reactions clearly labeled as fiction in every viewport; replace questionable public avatars with original typographic visual identities and reduce reading interference.
4. Evidence comparison: source-supported units and windows are separated, visitors cannot be linked to a named maker without identity evidence. Readers select records to inspect each claim's supported and unsupported conclusions.
5. People: public, attributed biographical paths; date ranges and identity continuity are explicit. Craft experience explains compressed actions and time. It cannot be counted as transmission.
6. Verdict: distinguish established makers' continuation from unknown visitor continuation; end on a human question and a concrete follow-up field guide, with AI as optional annex, not the editorial climax.
7. Annexes: national random map and long catalogue/roster/report tables no longer interrupt the main reading. Preserve their access and boundaries. Sources always accessible.

## Data architecture
Create `src/content/public-evidence.json` with metadata, sources, measures and people paths. Measures: id, label, valueText, unit, period, scope, sourceId, interpretation and limitation. People paths: stable id/name/craft, dated milestones each linked to source, interpretation and remainingUnknown. Include a bounded selected-source matrix whose flags reflect what a document explicitly supports, not an industry survey. Validate identifiers, source references, dates, no zero replacements for missing retention, and no causal claim.

## Experience and design
Use editorial typography, timeline rules, physical paper-like markers and source footnotes. No unrelated theme replacement or new dependencies. Main flow must get to evidence in the first three screens. At 390×900, the base reading should be materially shorter than the reviewed 20925px, aim <=16000px without hiding core evidence. Optional sections can grow after opening. Interaction controls >=44px where practical; no horizontal overflow at 320/390/768/1440. Source dialog focus and reduced motion preserved. Production must read without keys, external video, or WebGL; optional AI explains availability and handles failures.

## Required repairs
Physics/category/year transitions restore intended positions and stop old simulation. Mobile decorative comments cannot cover factual labels; fiction notice remains visible. Craft drawing precedes lengthy description on mobile. Video load failure has a legible fallback, with factual story independent of it. AI receives a bounded source context if configured, and local-only deployment limits are documented.

## Acceptance
Archive before and after. Public research register with original URLs, article dates, checked facts, limits. Production build passes. Data validation and evidence behavior tests pass. Existing browser/scroll/games/reports/effects/seal tests adapted for the intended final flow and run; external service tests explicitly separated from deterministic offline checks. Desktop/mobile screenshots inspected; keyboard/reduced-motion/source interactions checked. Independent final review resolves substantive findings. Save change log, verification and limitation records. Do not publish or alter shared git history.
